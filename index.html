How it works

Three steps, running continuously.

Gather and analyze

Threat intelligence is collected, weighted for reliability, then analyzed. The relationships between threats and what they target are derived and verified.

Build your organization profile

We combine what we discover about your attack surface with what you tell us: the technology behind it, the third parties you rely on, your sector and where you operate.

Alert where they meet

A threat against something you use, or one that matches your profile, becomes an alert, internet-facing or not. Exposure and exploitability decide how loud it is.

Every alert, defensible

Nothing to take on trust.

Each alert carries the chain of evidence that produced it, so you have everything you need to act on it with confidence, in a minute.

For organizations that refuse to be the last to know.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked.

What do you need from us to start?

Your domain. You can also declare the technology you use internally and a few facts about your organization: where you operate, your sector and the third parties you rely on.

Do you install anything in our network?

No agents, no integration, no installation. We discover what your organization exposes to the internet from the outside, the way an attacker would see it, without stress testing, penetration testing or anything intrusive.

How is this different from a threat intelligence feed?

A feed sends every subscriber the same reports and leaves the matching to your team. ThreatRaven does the matching, so you only hear about threats that reach what you run.

How do we know an alert is right?

Every alert carries its evidence: the intelligence, the match, and the part of your organization it concerns, all on one view you can check in a minute.

Who can see our data?

Your team, and the ThreatRaven staff who run your service. Your profile and your alerts are never shared with other customers.
